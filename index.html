> How to use this tool

This is a private notebook. Data stays in this browser unless you download a backup.

Rule one — no more zero days. A zero day is a day with nothing toward the person you want to be. One box, one sentence, one bag of trash is legal. You do not have to do the essay.

Present you is the only one who can act. Past you already tried. Future you lives with today.

If you blow it, forgive and start at the next hour. Not next Monday.

  • Set the three scales. That is weather, not a verdict.
  • 1–3 means shrink the plan and consider real support.
  • 4–6 is the baseline we protect.
  • 7–8 is a solid target. 9–10 is rare on purpose.
  • Tick care items when you do them. Points only describe the day.
  • Touch a goal if you moved it an inch.
  • Weight, calories, exercise are just numbers. No commentary.

A non-zero day kept the light on. A full send day emptied the tank. Tomorrow may be smaller. That is allowed.
